# Feature Specification: Checklist Item Owners & Due Dates

**Feature Branch**: `028-checklist-owner-due-date`

**Created**: 2026-09-28

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "Backlog #3 — checklist item owner + due date, turning the AI's phased checklist from advisory text into an assignable, trackable task list."

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Assigning an owner and a due date (Priority: P1) 🎯 MVP

Each governance aspect's checklist (Immediate, Near-term, Long-term) lists what the client should do, but nothing says who is doing it or by when. "Near-term" isn't a date. A consultant needs to assign each item to someone at the client and give it a real deadline.

**Why this priority**: This is the whole gap: without an owner and a date, the checklist is advice, not a plan.

**Independent Test**: Assign a checklist item to a role and give it a due date, then assign another to a named person. Confirm both show their owner and date.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a checklist item, **When** an Editor assigns it an owner (a role or a person from the workspace's directory, not both) and a due date, **Then** the item shows both.
2. **Given** an item with an owner or due date, **When** an Editor changes or clears either, **Then** the change is kept.
3. **Given** a checklist item added by hand, or generated by an assessment, **When** it's first created, **Then** it has no owner and no due date until someone sets them. Nothing is guessed from its phase.

---

### User Story 2 - Seeing what's overdue (Priority: P2)

Once items have dates, the consultant needs to see which ones have slipped.

**Why this priority**: It depends on User Story 1's dates existing, but it's what makes the dates useful.

**Independent Test**: Give one open item a due date in the past and one Done item a due date in the past. Confirm only the open one is flagged Overdue.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an item that isn't Done or Dismissed and whose due date has passed, **When** viewing the checklist, **Then** it's flagged Overdue.
2. **Given** a Done or Dismissed item past its due date, **When** viewing the checklist, **Then** it's not flagged.
3. **Given** an aspect's checklist, **When** viewing it, **Then** the number of overdue items is shown alongside the aspect's tab or heading.

---

### Edge Cases

- What happens to an item's owner when that role or person is archived (this app archives roles and people rather than deleting them)? The owner stays on the item, shown with "(archived)", and can be reassigned. Archived roles and people aren't offered for new assignments.
- What happens when an assessment is regenerated? Existing items (and their owners and dates) are untouched. A regenerate only ever adds items whose titles it hasn't seen before, and it never revisits existing ones.
- What happens when an item's phase is changed? Its due date stays as set. Phase and due date are independent.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: System MUST let an Editor set, change, or clear a checklist item's owner, being either a role or a person from the workspace's directory, never both.
- **FR-002**: System MUST let an Editor set, change, or clear a checklist item's due date.
- **FR-003**: A newly created item, whether added by hand or generated, MUST start with no owner and no due date.
- **FR-004**: An item that isn't Done or Dismissed and is past its due date MUST be flagged Overdue. A Done or Dismissed item MUST NOT be.
- **FR-005**: Each aspect's overdue-item count MUST be visible alongside that aspect.
- **FR-006**: An item whose owner is archived MUST keep that owner, shown as archived. Archived roles and people MUST NOT be offered when assigning. Deleting a role or person outright (which only happens when a whole workspace is deleted) MUST NOT delete the item.
- **FR-007**: Regenerating an assessment MUST NOT change any existing item's owner or due date.
- **FR-008**: Owner and due-date fields MUST be readable by Viewers and above, and writable by Editors and above.

### Key Entities

- **Checklist item** (existing, extended): gains an optional owner (role or person) and an optional due date. Its overdue state is derived, not stored.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: Every checklist item can be assigned to someone and given a real deadline.
- **SC-002**: A consultant can see every overdue checklist item, per aspect, without opening each one.
- **SC-003**: Zero owners or due dates are ever lost to an assessment regenerate.

## Assumptions

- No reminders or notifications for due or overdue items.
- Owners are drawn from the workspace's existing roles and People directory, the same choice the Risk Register already offers.
- No bulk assignment in this version; each item is assigned individually.
